Pakistan's favourite card game, online. Four players, two teams, partners sit opposite. First team to 7 of 13 tricks wins the hand.

How to play

Playing together

  • One person taps Create game and shares the 5-letter code (or the invite link). Everyone else enters the code.
  • Seats opposite each other are partners. Tap Sit here to pick your seat. Empty seats are played by the computer, so 2 or 3 people can play too.
  • Every new game gets a new code. If someone leaves in the middle, the computer takes their seat. They can come back with the code and they'll be seated when the next game starts.

The deal

  • Play moves anticlockwise. The player to the dealer's right gets five cards first and calls the rang (trump suit) from those alone. Then everyone is dealt up to 13.

Tricks

  • The rang caller leads the first trick. You must follow the suit that was led if you can.
  • If you can't, play anything — a rang card cuts the trick. Highest rang wins; otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins. Aces are high.
  • Whoever wins a trick leads the next one. You have 60 seconds per move — after that the computer plays for you.

Scoring

  • First team to 7 tricks wins the hand: 1 point.
  • If a team reaches 7 before the other has taken a single trick, play continues. Take all 13 and it's a Court: 3 points.
  • If the rang caller's team loses the hand, the deal moves one seat anticlockwise. If they win, the same dealer deals again.
